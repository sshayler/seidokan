The art of Iaido

A tradition carried with care

Seidokan Dojo is the only registered branch of the Toyama Ryu Iaido Kai outside of Japan. Learn to handle and test cut with the famous samurai sword. Toyama Ryu has no seated techniques and is suitable for adults aged 18 and over (16 by discretion).

Complete syllabus

The only dojo teaching the full Morinaga Ha Toyama Ryu syllabus.

Start at your pace

Your first lesson is free. Wear loose-fitting clothing; equipment can be provided in the short term. You are welcome to watch with no obligation.
